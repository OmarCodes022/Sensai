title: Soft Focus Eye Cream

Soft Focus Eye Cream is Serein Studio's cream for the eye area. Its texture is light and cooling. It visibly reduces puffiness in 7 days. Tap a rice-grain amount around each eye with your ring finger, morning and evening, without touching the eyelid. It is fragrance-free and ophthalmologist-tested.
