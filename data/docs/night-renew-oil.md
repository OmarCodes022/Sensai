title: Night Renew Oil

Night Renew Oil is Serein Studio's night face oil made with squalane. Its texture is silky and it absorbs without leaving the skin greasy. Skin feels softer after 2 weeks. Warm 3 drops between your palms and press them on a clean face each night, after your serum. It is fragrance-free.
