title: Velvet Cleansing Balm
---

Velvet Cleansing Balm is Serein Studio's melting cleansing balm. It turns into a light oil on contact with the skin, then into a milk when you add water. It removes long-wear makeup in one step. Massage one scoop on a dry face in the evening, add a little water and rinse. It can also be used in the morning as a gentle cleanse. It is fragrance-free.
