Dew Veil SPF 50 is Serein Studio's daily sunscreen fluid. It gives broad-spectrum SPF 50 protection. Its texture is invisible, it leaves no white cast and works well under makeup. Apply it every morning as the last step of your routine, two finger-lengths for the face and neck. Reapply every two hours when you are outside.
